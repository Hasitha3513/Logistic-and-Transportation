# Phase-2 Scope & Traceability Matrix (PHASE2-SCOPE-MATRIX)

**Release Baseline:** `v1.0.0-rc.1` (RC-1 Certified)  
**Target Delivery:** Phase-2 Route Intelligence & Commercial Freight Operations  
**Evaluation Date:** August 24, 2026  

> **PHASE2-SCOPE-RECON-001 — RESOLVED AND FROZEN (August 25, 2026):** The authoritative Transportation & Logistics source defines US-29 as **Generate Freight Reports** and US-30 as **Handle Cargo Exceptions**. It also defines US-26 as **Plan Loads** and US-27 as **Validate Weight and Volume**. Freight charges belong to **US-47 — Manage Transport Billing**, outside US-24 through US-30. See `PHASE2-SCOPE-RECONCILIATION-001.md`. The prior mismatch is preserved in that document's historical note.
